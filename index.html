About moucharabi

moucharabi is a web-based platform designed to help creators manage and publish video content through supported social media integrations.

TikTok Integration

moucharabi allows authorized users to connect their TikTok account and use supported TikTok features to upload and publish video content.

Users explicitly authorize access through TikTok's official authorization process. moucharabi does not access a user's TikTok account without authorization.
